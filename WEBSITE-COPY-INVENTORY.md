### FAQ section

- Existing heading: "THE QUESTIONS YOU’RE PROBABLY THINKING."
- Intro paragraph:
  - "No pressure and no promise of overnight results. Just clear answers about what a site can—and can’t—do for your business."

#### Business and website FAQs
- "Most of my work comes from recommendations. Why do I need a website?"
  - "Because even a recommendation often gets checked online before someone calls. A clear, professional site backs up the referral, answers the obvious questions and gives new customers a way to find you too. It works while you're on the tools, after hours and when you're busy."
- "I already have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?"
  - "Keep using it—it can be great for updates and reviews. But a website gives you a professional home you control, with your services, service area, work and contact details together in one place. Customers don't have to scroll through a feed or message and wait to find out if you do their kind of job."
- "Will a website get me to the top of Google?"
  - "No one can honestly guarantee that. I build clear service and location information with sound technical foundations, so customers and search engines can understand what you offer. Your Google Business Profile, reviews and local competition matter too."
- "What should my website actually do for my business?"
  - "Make the decision to contact you easier. It should show what work you take on, where you cover, examples of your work and the quickest way to call or request a quote. That means fewer basic questions and better-informed enquiries—not a promise of a set number of jobs."
- "Do I own the site?"
  - "Yes. Your words, images and site are yours. The handover is clear, and there is no mystery lock-in."
- "What if I need changes after launch?"
  - "Small tweaks are normal. We can agree a simple support arrangement, or I can leave you with a straightforward handover for the bits you want to update."

#### Additional FAQ copy
- "Websites sound expensive. What does the price cover?"
  - "A one-page Starter Website is £270, and a 3–5 page Complete Website is £400. I’ll explain what’s included and any additional costs before work begins, so you can decide without pressure."
- "I don’t have time to manage a website."
  - "The site is built around the information customers need, not another stream of posts. We’ll agree what should be on it before launch. If you want help with changes afterwards, we can discuss a support arrangement."
- "I don’t know anything about websites. Is that a problem?"
  - "Not at all. You know your trade and your customers; I’ll explain the choices in plain English and handle the build. You can review the content and ask questions before anything goes live."
- "What does basic SEO setup mean?"
  - "Basic SEO setup helps search engines understand what your website and services are about. This includes descriptive page titles and descriptions, clear page headings, relevant service and location information, a sitemap and essential indexing setup. It provides a technical SEO foundation but does not guarantee Google rankings or specific search positions."
